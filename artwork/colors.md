# Cin-MinAI colours

Taken from Ian's logo and concept art (2026-09-27): black space, electric blue, neon green, and the
deep teal of circuit traces between them. Sampled from `logo/logo-ian-original.png` and
`wallpapers/concepts/`.

| Name | Hex | From | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signal blue** | `#00A4EC` | logo oval | the brand colour: accents, selected items, the menu button, links |
| **Neon green** | `#18D42C` | logo rim | highlights and "ready / OK" states, sparingly (it glows) |
| **Void** | `#000000` | logo background, boot splash | boot screens, the darkest surfaces |
| **Deep panel** | `#02141C` | concept art, dark areas | panel and window backgrounds in the dark theme |
| **Circuit teal** | `#106979` | concept art, mid-tones | borders, inactive accents |
| **Glow cyan** | `#34B5B8` | concept art, light traces | hover and focus glow |
| **Text on blue** | `#000000` | logo lettering | text on Signal blue (as in the logo) |
| **Text on dark** | `#E6F4F8` | chosen for contrast | normal text on Deep panel |

Contrast (WCAG): black on Signal blue ≈ 7.5:1, Neon green on black ≈ 10.5:1, `#E6F4F8` on Deep panel ≈ 17:1 —
all well above the 4.5:1 needed for readable text.

Fonts: the logo's lettering is a bold condensed grotesque (Helvetica/Franklin family). Those fonts can't be
redistributed, so the vector logo uses the closest open-licence (OFL) font — chosen side by side with
Ian's original before use.
