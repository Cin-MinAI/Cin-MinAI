First, thank you for trying Cin-MinAI. I truly hope it helps you become familiar with Linux based environments. I was able to find my way through but it was not easy. However, on the other side of this journey is an OS that gives digital sovereignty to the user and a much more satisfying experience. This port was designed to ease that pain.
